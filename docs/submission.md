## Description (paste)

Thesis turns a belief about the future into a basket of 1–3 tokenized stocks on Solana, and lets
you gift it.

- **Gift a pack.** Pick a pack, write a note, send it to a friend's X handle, from $1. They open
  the link, rip a 3D foil pack open (no sign-in to look), then claim it with X. We create a wallet
  for them (Privy), and their USDC buys the stocks into their own wallet through Jupiter. No
  custody: we never hold funds or keys.
- **Pre-IPO, through PreStocks.** "Before they go public" holds OpenAI and Anthropic via PreStocks'
  tokens, with NVIDIA as the public reference price. Every screen says what pre-IPO means: no
  public share price, a 0.5% transfer fee, and issuer freeze/buy-back rights.
- **Every basket has a voice.** Baskets start from a real, credited post (Naval on AI liability,
  Brian Armstrong on IPO access), with the argument, the case against, and what would change the
  call. Each call is a public 90-day record against the S&P 500, measured from the day it
  started. We show no backtests as returns.
- **A real terminal.** Rank baskets by their live record. Holdings are always in view, read from
  your wallet on chain. The portfolio shows what each thesis you bought is worth today.
- **Honest numbers.** 12-month what-ifs are labelled as such; interest comes from Wikipedia reads,
  never invented counts; pre-IPO packs show their live record only.

Stack: Next.js on Vercel, Postgres (Neon), Jupiter for execution, Backed xStocks and PreStocks
tokens, Privy embedded wallets, three.js.
