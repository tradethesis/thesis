# Thesis — Give someone their first investment

Investor and partner narrative · 23 September 2026

Thesis turns an investment idea into a personal gift. Choose a themed stock-token pack, add a budget and note, and address it to a friend's X account. The intended recipient experience is to sign in with X, meet eligibility requirements, and open that gift into a wallet they control.

A first investment asks a newcomer to choose assets, learn a new product, and commit their own money. Our hypothesis is that a recognizable future and a trusted sender make that first step easier. The sender is already an investor; the recipient may never have used a wallet.

The thesis makes the gift meaningful. The pack makes receiving it memorable. Holdings and weights are disclosed: this is a fixed allocation with an enjoyable reveal, not a lottery or randomized payout. The investment can lose value.

xStocks supplies stock exposure onchain. Thesis aims to supply distribution and onboarding. The product starts with existing stock-token allocations, USDC, and Solana wallets. It does not issue a speculative basket token. Stock tokens are not stablecoins or direct shareholder ownership, and availability depends on the market and distributor.

Stock gifting already exists. Our proposed wedge combines personal investment narratives, X recipient identity, and wallet-based delivery. This is not an established moat. Trustworthy claims, useful packs and repeat senders are the advantage to earn.

The business hypothesis is a transparent sender-paid service fee, disclosed separately from the gift budget and execution costs. We need to prove that it covers execution, identity, support and failed-claim costs. No fee rate or revenue is claimed.

The first proposed pilot is 20 self-funded senders, three reviewed packs, and one verified eligible market. Measure paid gifts, completed claims, support burden, cost per claimed gift, recipient self-funded investments and repeat gifting. A preview open is not activation. The decisive evidence is a second decision made with the user's own money.

Today the local build supports pack selection, budget and note composition, allocation review, a shareable unfunded preview, and a mobile pack reveal. Optional X sign-in is present; it is not verified gift-claim authorization. Funding, stable X identity verification, delivery, refunds, recovery and the recipient's usable exit remain required before a live pilot.
